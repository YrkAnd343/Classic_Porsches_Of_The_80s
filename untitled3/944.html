Porsche 944

The Porsche 944 was a relatively cheap Porsche when it was being made, this made it popular amongst those who enjoyed Porsche but couldn't afford a 911 or 959

The Porsche 944 was Equipped with a 2L 4-cylinder engine instead of the typical 6-cylinder engines Porsches are known for. although the 944 is still RWD so its quite the capable sports car
